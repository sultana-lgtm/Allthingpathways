# Easy Routine (the short version)

Goal: fewer decisions, no cooking, enough protein and fiber, no burnout.
If the full plan (`daily-routine.md`) feels like too much, use only this page.

## The 5 daily anchors (that's the whole routine)

| # | Anchor | Keep it simple |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fajr**, then water and lights on | Just pray and drink water |
| 2 | **Breakfast shake** (protein + fiber, 2 min) | Just the shake |
| 3 | **Protein + fiber at lunch** (ready meal or default lunch) | Anything from the default list below |
| 4 | **Dhuhr / Asr as breaks** away from the screen | Stand up and stretch |
| 5 | **Lights out around 10 pm**, phone across the room | Screens off, get into bed |

Work days: teach Tue/Thu, admin Mon/Wed/Fri. Gym is a bonus: two easy sessions a week is plenty.

## The no-cook food system

Rule: **stop deciding what to eat. Have a default for each meal.**

### Breakfast: the shake (same every day)
- 1 scoop vegan protein (strawberry cream) + a plant milk or water
- Add **1 tbsp ground flax or chia** and **a handful of frozen berries** for fiber
- Optional: half a banana or a spoon of oats
- Takes 2 minutes, and you can drink it in the car or on the way to the shop

### Lunch: ready meals (Cookt or similar)
- Order weekly. Keep 5-6 in the freezer or fridge so there's always one available.
- Choose ones with **20 g+ protein and some beans, lentils, veg or whole grains**.
- Teaching days: pack one, plus fruit or a snack. Admin days: eat at the shop.

### Dinner: assemble, don't cook
Pick any 3 from each column. No real cooking is needed:

| Protein | Fiber / veg | Base |
|---|---|---|
| Canned chickpeas or lentils | Bagged salad or pre-cut veg | Microwave rice or quinoa pouch |
| Tofu (pre-baked), tempeh | Frozen veg (steam in the microwave) | Wholemeal wraps or bread |
| Hummus | Fruit | Ready meal from the freezer |
| Edamame | Canned beans | Instant noodles/oats (with extras) |

Examples: hummus wrap with salad, ready meal plus frozen veg, microwave rice with edamame and beans.

### Snacks (protein and fiber, no prep)
- Fruit plus nut butter, roasted chickpeas, edamame, dates and nuts, a second protein shake, oat bars with 8 g+ protein

### When you eat out (it will happen, and that's fine)
- Pick the option with **beans, lentils, veg or a protein** on it.
- Add a side salad or veg if there is one.
- Have your **shake first** so you're not starving when you order.
- Keep two go-to places and orders saved in your phone.

## Weekly setup: 30 minutes, once
Do this on Sunday or another quiet time:
1. Order the ready meals and top up the shake ingredients (powder, flax/chia, frozen berries, plant milk).
2. Restock the dinner list above: canned beans, rice pouches, wraps, frozen veg, hummus.
3. Pick which 3 ready meals you'll take on Tuesday and Thursday and pack the bag.
4. Write your top 3 admin tasks for Monday.

## Each day's two check-ins (30 seconds each)
- **After Fajr:** pick your top 3 tasks for the day. That's it.
- **Before bed:** Did I get protein and some fiber today? Set the shake for tomorrow.

## What to ignore for now
Everything in the full plan that isn't on this page. Add things back only after 2 steady weeks. Order to add them: gym, stretching, the Sunday weekly reset, meal variety.
